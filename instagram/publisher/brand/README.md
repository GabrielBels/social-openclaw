# Logo for SVG slides

`render.mjs` adds one of these to every slide it draws from SVG. The files here are neutral placeholders: replace them with the brand's own logo, cropped to its marks, 960 pixels wide and no taller than about 450, as PNG with a transparent background.

- `logo-light.png`: the dark wordmark, for marfim backgrounds and for the marfim badge on verde and cobre.
- `logo-dark.png`: the white wordmark, for azul backgrounds.

The fonts in `../fonts/` are Geist and Inter, with their SIL Open Font License texts.
